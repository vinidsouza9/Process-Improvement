# Operations & Business Process Management — Case Studies

## Overview

This is a single-page site combining two case studies for Operations & Business Process Management graduate coursework project:

- **Team Project - FedEx Clearance Loop**: A customs clearance backlog case study using Theory of Constraints and Little's Law, with a live queueing simulation.
- **Individual Project - University Headshot Event**: A queueing/bottleneck case study on a university photography event, with its own interactive simulation comparing scheduling and staffing scenarios.

**Theory of Constraints**: Any process has one bottleneck that limits how much it can produce overall, improving anything else doesn't help until that constraint is fixed. The approach is to find the bottleneck, get the most out of it, align everything else around it, then work on loosening it.

**Little's Law**: A queueing formula, *L = λW*, the average number of items waiting in a system equals the arrival rate multiplied by the average time each item spends in the system. It connects arrival rate and time-in-system to how big a queue gets.
